### **Website Specifications**

**Static Pages & Content Requirements**

* **A. Home Page (The Welcome Hub)**  
  * **Header:** Features the official seal of Barangay San Antonio de Padua I. Includes the main navigation menu.  
  * **Hero Section:** Displays a high-quality image of the Day Care Center. Features a welcoming headline such as "Welcome to Barangay San Antonio de Padua I Day Care Center."  
  * **Quick Summary:** A brief welcoming statement highlighting the center's dedication to early childhood education, community support, and creating a fun, nurturing environment for the children of the barangay.  
  * **Action Button:** Features a prominently displayed "Staff Login" button for personnel access.  
* **B. About Us (History & Mission)**  
  * **Mission:** "To provide a safe, nurturing, and stimulating educational environment that fosters the holistic development, well-being, and foundational learning of the children in our community."  
  * **Vision:** "To be a leading community partner in early childhood education, ensuring that every child in Barangay San Antonio de Padua I is empowered, cared for, and prepared for lifelong learning."  
  * **Faculty Profiles:** Introduces the dedicated daycare personnel, such as Ms. Cherry. This feature aims to build trust and familiarity with the parents.  
  * **Community Impact:** Explains how the center's programs and daily activities actively support child development, prepare children for primary school, and engage with parents to build a strong community foundation.  
* **C. Announcement Board (Parent Portal)**  
  * **Real-time Updates:** Serves as an active feed for school events, holiday notices, and parent-teacher meeting schedules.  
  * **Health Alerts:** Provides specific reminders for scheduled tasks. This includes important alerts for child weight monitoring.  
  * **Transparency:** Ensures consistent communication regarding school activities and child well-being.  
* **D. Contact & Location**  
  * **Barangay Info:** Displays the official address: Barangay San Antonio de Padua I, Dasmariñas City, Cavite. Includes the official contact numbers.  
  * **Office Hours:** Lists the standard operating hours for the daycare center.  
  * **Messenger Integration:** Provides a direct link or button to the center’s Messenger for quick inquiries. This integration is used because the personnel are already familiar with the tool.  
* **E. Login Page (The Gateway)**  
  * **Security Disclaimer:** Contains a strict notice stating that the portal is for authorized personnel only. This ensures the protection of sensitive student data.  
  * **Form:** Includes simple "Username" and "Password" fields.  
  * **Navigation:** Provides a helpful link back to the public homepage in case a parent or public user reached the page by mistake.

  ### **Website Flow (User Journey)**

The flow ensures a clear separation between public viewing of the daycare's information and private administrative tasks for the teachers.

* **Entry:** The user arrives at the Home Page.  
* **Information Phase:** The user, who is likely a parent, navigates to the About Us or Announcement Board sections to check for school updates.  
* **Authentication Phase:** A staff member clicks the Login button.  
* **Verification:** The system securely validates the entered credentials.  
* **Success:** The authorized staff member gains access to manage the center's private records and daily operations.
