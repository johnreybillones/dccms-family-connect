1. **Community Problem**

Currently, the Barangay San Antonio de Padua I Day Care Center encounters significant difficulties in relation to its handling and management of day-to-day data. This information can be associated with an inefficient and outdated manual data flow to which its staff is highly reliant on..

Through meetings and interviews with the day care employees, the following problems were observed:

- **Slow and Manual Student Information Retrieval.** Records of every student are handwritten and recorded in physical log books and cabinets. Retrieval of single records, especially that of a single student, is so slow that it takes one up to one hour per record. Moreover, the possibility of damage, loss and incompleteness of records increases.
- **No Provided Computers, Slow Connectivity.** The day care center is unable to accommodate its own computer system. The employees are made to ask other barangay day care centers for loaned computers simply for data entry or report submission resulting in long delays. In addition to this, it has poor internet connectivity.
- **High Data Security Risks.** Data is scattered all throughout due to lack of centralized databases. Highly sensitive personal data about the children are in danger due to the nature of their placement within unlocked physical cabinets, or in portable media that could be loaned to others, among other ways they're unsecured.
- **Limited digital literacy/technical support.** Personnel are generally not digitally inclined and feels the prior training they underwent was not sufficient enough for them to be confident with new technologies; they do not have a point for them to turn to for technical difficulties. No proper support can even be expected from the barangay itself.

All in all, these problems cause many significant delays in activities such as reporting, documentation, and curriculum development. The old system eats up a lot of the workers' valuable time in reporting and document keeping, which takes them away from actual lesson planning. Hence, an innovative solution is highly necessary.

**The Scope of the Computer System or Digital Solution**  
The Day Care Center Management System (DCCMS) is a web-based application designed to digitize the daily operations of the Day Care Center of Barangay San Antonio de Padua I. The system focuses on two main areas: providing a general overview of the center and allowing authorized personnel to log and manage student information in a centralized database. It is designed to be simple, offline-capable, and accessible to users with limited digital experience.  
Student record management is the core of the system. It replaces paper logbooks by allowing personnel to store and retrieve complete student profiles quickly and securely. Attendance logging simplifies the daily task of tracking student presence, reducing missed or inaccurate entries. Report generation automates the production of accomplishment reports and databases, so personnel only need to review and submit the output rather than manually encode everything. Offline access ensures these features remain usable even without internet, which is a non-negotiable requirement given the center's connectivity issues. The dashboard gives a quick overview of the center's current status, the announcement page provides a simple way to share updates with parents, and the login system ensures sensitive data is only accessible to authorized users.

4. **Recommendations**

Based on the findings and the development of the Day Care Center Management System (DCCMS), the following recommendations are proposed to ensure the system's long-term effectiveness and sustainability:

**For the Day Care Center Personnel**

It is recommended that all staff undergo a structured and hands-on orientation session prior to the full deployment of the system. Training should be tailored to their current skill level, focusing on the most frequently used features such as attendance logging, student record retrieval, and report generation. A printed quick-reference guide should also be provided so that personnel can troubleshoot minor issues independently without relying on external support.

**For the Barangay Officials**

The barangay is encouraged to allocate at least one dedicated computer unit for the exclusive use of the day care center. This will eliminate the dependency on borrowed devices and significantly reduce delays in data encoding and report submission. Investing in a stable internet connection, even a basic one, would further enhance the system's synchronization and backup capabilities.

**For Future Developers**

Should the system be expanded or handed over to future development teams, it is recommended to consider adding a native mobile application to allow personnel greater flexibility in accessing records. Integration with CSWD regional reporting formats should also be explored to further streamline the submission process. Regular system maintenance and data backup schedules should be established to prevent data loss.

**For the Academic Institution**

Continued community engagement programs of this nature should be supported and encouraged, as they provide students with practical experience while delivering meaningful solutions to underserved communities. Future batches may build upon this project to add more advanced features as the day care center's digital capacity grows.
